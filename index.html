🔵 What is Blob Cloaking?

Blob cloaking is a trick used by unblockers to hide the real website you're visiting by loading it through a special type of URL called a blob: URL.

🕵️ How It Works

It's mainly meant to confuse school filters like GoGuardian, Securly, LanSchool, etc., because those systems usually track:

  • The real URL you're on
  • The tab title
  • The domain in your address bar

Blob cloaking messes with all three.

🔗 What is a "blob:" URL?

A blob URL looks like this:

blob:https://yourdomain.com/83d7fd8d-2c80-4efd-9c27-1b929c87fd1e

Notice something? 👉 It doesn't show the real site inside it.

Instead, it points to a temporary "file/object" stored in the browser. Filters like GoGuardian only see your domain, not what's actually running.

🔥 What Blob Cloaking Does

  1. Makes the site run inside a blob - So instead of seeing the real site URL, filters only see blob:https://yourdomain.com/randomid
  2. Hides the favicon + tab title - Your tab looks like Google Docs, Canvas, etc.
  3. Makes it appear as your own site - GoGuardian sees your domain, your title, your favicon

⚠️ Important Reality Check

Blob cloaking is NOT perfect and CAN be detected by:

  • Screen monitoring (GoGuardian Teacher mode)
  • Network traffic analysis
  • Keyword detection
  • Extension-level inspection

It's not "magic invisibility," but it's one of the best obfuscation techniques used today.
